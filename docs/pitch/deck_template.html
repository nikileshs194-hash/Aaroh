Why current options fail

Cloud AI

Needs internet that a third of schools lack. Children's data leaves the premises. Cost grows with every question.

Local AI today

Needs an expert to pick the right model and settings for each old PC. Defaults are tuned for one user.

Nothing plans for a class

Works for one student, crawls when twenty join. Nobody can say how many students a PC can serve, or whether Kannada answers are any good.

Developer tools (llama-bench, VectorPrime, Ollama, LM Studio) tune or run one model for one user. Cloud copilots need internet. InferSmart is ongoing serving for a whole class, set up by a teacher. Models from India and the world are our supply chain, not our rivals.

How it works
Architecture

Phones and lab PCs → school Wi-Fi → control centre (Python, FastAPI) → engine A quality model, engine B fast model (llama.cpp, CPU). One SQLite file. Nothing needs the internet.

Quality guard + capacity

Each setting is checked against its 8-bit reference on Belebele (EN/HI/KN) and ARC-Easy questions, and must stay above a floor in every chosen language. Then 1 to 12 virtual students: N = most students with ≥ 5 tokens/s and first word ≤ 3 s.

Three-mode controller

Every 2 s: observe users, queue, first word, speed, memory. Limits broken for 20 s → quality → fast → protect. Calm for 60 s → back. Every decision written in plain words.

Impact and business model
Theory of change

Existing PC + InferSmart (free) → automatic setup → private offline AI for teachers and students → lesson plans in local languages, no data leaving school, AI curriculum delivered without internet → fairer access to AI for government and rural schools.

Aligned with SDG 4 and 10, NEP 2020, the 2026-27 AI curriculum, DPDP data minimisation, and Karnataka's KEO AI-PC programme.

Sustainability

Free open-source core for schools. Income from paid deployment and teacher training for state and CSR programmes, annual support, and pre-installation with low-cost AI-PC makers.

Zero marginal cost: an extra question costs the electricity of a PC that is already on. No cloud bills.
